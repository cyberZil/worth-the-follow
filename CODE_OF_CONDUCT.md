# Code of Conduct

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)
in full. The summary below is for convenience; the Covenant text is the authoritative version.

## In short

We are building a tool that helps people step away from spaces that drain them. The project itself
should be the opposite of that.

**Expected:** be kind and direct; assume good faith; give feedback on the work, not the person; accept
that values differ across cultures and communities and that a lens which feels obvious to you may not
to someone else; credit others; take responsibility and apologise when you get it wrong.

**Not tolerated:** harassment of any kind; slurs or demeaning comments about anyone's identity;
sexualised language or attention; doxxing or publishing others' private information; sustained
disruption; and — specific to this project — **proposing lenses that target people for who they are
rather than for what they post.** See CONTRIBUTING.md on protected characteristics.

## Reporting

Report conduct issues privately to the maintainers via the email listed on the repository's GitHub
profile. Reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers will acknowledge within a week and follow the
Covenant's enforcement guidelines (correction → warning → temporary ban → permanent ban), proportionate
to the behaviour.

## Scope

This applies in every project space — issues, PRs, discussions, and any public space where someone is
representing the project.
